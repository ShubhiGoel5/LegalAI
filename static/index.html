You

My employment contract says I can't join a competitor for 2 years after I resign. Is that enforceable?

⚖️ Legal AI Agent

Generally, no. A restriction that applies after your employment ends is usually unenforceable in India.

  • Agreements that restrain anyone from carrying on a lawful profession, trade or business are void under Section 27.
  • Indian courts have consistently refused to enforce post-employment non-compete clauses. Restrictions during employment are usually upheld.
  • Confidentiality obligations and protection of trade secrets can still be enforced after you leave.
